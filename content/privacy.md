_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

This website is run by Lohn. It is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

## What is collected

**Contact form.** When you send a message, your name, email address and message are emailed to me together with your IP address and browser type (used to prevent abuse). The form does not store your message in a database.

**Spam protection.** The form uses [ALTCHA](https://altcha.org), a privacy-friendly check that runs in your browser. It sets no cookies and sends nothing to third parties.

**Server logs.** Like any web server, this site records technical access logs (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type). They are used for security and troubleshooting and are rotated automatically.

## Advertising on Notes articles

Articles in the [Notes](/notes/) section may show ads from **Google AdSense**. The home page, the contact form and this page never show ads, and the rest of this site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

On an article that shows ads:

- Google and other ad companies working with Google may use cookies, or similar technology, to show ads based on your earlier visits to this site and to other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners choose ads using those visits.
- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message from Google asks for your choice before cookies are used for personalised ads. You can change your choice later from the link that message leaves on the page.
- You can turn off personalised ads from Google in [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), and from many other ad companies at [aboutads.info](https://optout.aboutads.info/). You will still see ads, but they won't be based on your browsing.
- How Google uses data from sites that show its ads: [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

I don't receive your personal data from Google; AdSense only reports totals (for example, how many ads were shown).

## Affiliate links

Some articles may contain affiliate links. They are marked, and clicking one takes you to the partner's website, which has its own privacy policy.

## Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, by emailing [lohn@lohn.cc](mailto:lohn@lohn.cc).
