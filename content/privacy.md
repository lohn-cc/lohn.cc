_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

This website is run by Lohn. It is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

## What is collected

**Contact form.** When you send a message, your name, email address and message are emailed to me together with your IP address and browser type (used to prevent abuse). The form does not store your message in a database.

**Spam protection.** The form uses [ALTCHA](https://altcha.org), a privacy-friendly check that runs in your browser. It sets no cookies and sends nothing to third parties.

**Server logs.** Like any web server, this site records technical access logs (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type). They are used for security and troubleshooting and are rotated automatically.

## Cookies and tracking

This site currently sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Some articles may contain affiliate links; these are marked, and clicking one takes you to the partner's website, which has its own privacy policy.

If advertising is added in the future, this page will be updated first and you will be asked for consent where required.

## Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, by emailing [lohn@lohn.cc](mailto:lohn@lohn.cc).
